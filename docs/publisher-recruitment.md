# Publisher contact records

These helpers support users who choose to organize permissioned testing with other publishers. They contain no contact list, proposed target, or actual engagement record.

Use the blank `templates/publisher-fit-check.md` to record actual suitability and interest privately. A prospect, draft invitation, or positive fit review is not permission to change a project, access a device, or publish evidence.

The lifecycle distinguishes screening, invited, interest-recorded, declined, paused, and screened-out. Record interest only from an explicit positive reply. Silence or social-media reactions do not establish agreement.

`cohort prepare-register` can create a new empty private register outside Git. The register stores opaque references and record digests, not names, contact routes, app IDs, private project URLs, or invitation text.

`cohort record-prospect-review` records a human decision on exact prospect and draft bytes. Send-decision preparation and recording are separate steps. None of these commands transports a message. See [prospect review](publisher-prospect-review.md), [send preparation](publisher-send-decision-preparation.md), and [send recording](publisher-send-decision-recording.md).

Permission for a test session and a later evidence-sharing decision must be obtained separately even when a person expresses interest.
