Limitations

  • This package contains self-recorded fresh-session evidence and no independent attestation.
  • The reports used an ordinary physical Android device with reference or mock MWA wallets.
  • Controlled broken and fixed fixtures do not establish defects in an external publisher project.
  • The observed flows do not establish a production wallet, Seed Vault, Seeker hardware, or an on-chain transaction.
  • Stable private source SHA-256 values provide integrity bindings and may allow correlation by anyone who already has the private source bytes.
  • This package may support a grant application, but it does not evaluate submission eligibility or establish an award.
